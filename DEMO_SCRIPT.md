## Full script — copy and paste

```text
Every day, scammers disguise themselves as limited-time deals, delivery alerts, and five-star reviews. The pressure is designed to make you act before you think.

ScamShield AI is the pause button. Paste a suspicious link, review, or message, and our Java-powered engine returns a clear zero-to-one-hundred threat score in under a second — no account, no data stored, and no black box.

Watch the ninety-percent-off deal. ScamShield scores it one hundred out of one hundred: DANGER. Then it shows why — an unverified domain, gift-card payment, extreme discount, and manufactured urgency — and gives three safe actions.

Now the official Amazon store. Zero out of one hundred: SAFE. The verified domain and clean language checks appear instantly, so legitimate shopping doesn't get punished.

ScamShield turns cybersecurity into a consumer product: explainable, instant, private, and dependency-free. For B2C AI, that's the win — trust people can see, at the moment it matters.
```

## Delivery notes

- Speak at a calm, confident pace. Pause briefly after “DANGER” and “SAFE.”
- Emphasize the numbers: **one hundred**, **zero**, **under a second**.
- Let the final line land slowly; the end card holds for eight seconds.
- The video is intentionally silent. Record the voiceover separately, then align it so “DANGER” lands as the red 100/100 meter finishes.
